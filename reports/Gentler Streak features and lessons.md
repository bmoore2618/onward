# Gentler Streak wins on tone, loses on strength

Gentler Streak is a Slovenian, eight-person, bootstrapped Apple Watch app that replaced the "close your rings every day" chain with a green band of healthy activity you try to stay inside, where a rest day is a step on the path rather than a hole in it. That single reframing, delivered in warm plain-language copy and compared only against your own history, is what Apple's editors cited when they gave it the 2024 Apple Design Award for Social Impact ("powered not by insistent reminders but an optimistic and encouraging vibe") ([Apple Design Awards 2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024)), and it is what carried the company past **€1M in trailing-year revenue and 45,100 subscribers by September 2023** with no ad spend ([Startup.si](https://www.startup.si/sl-si/novica/gentler-stories-septembra-presegel-milijon-prihodkov-v-zadnjih-365-dneh)). The product is built on three ideas Onward already shares: no streak you can lose by accident, a daily answer to "what should I do today?", and no accounts or servers. Where it is weak is exactly where Onward is strong: it has no structured program, no progression, and its heart-rate-based load model scores strength sessions as too light, the most repeated complaint in its reviews. The right move for Onward is therefore not to become Gentler Streak but to borrow its four best mechanics (a "life happens" status that silences the app, a one-sentence interpretation above everything else, a visible "what if I rest" preview, and recaps that compare you to yourself), keep its own calendar-anchored 75-day structure and explicit set-and-weight logging, and avoid the floating-band model, the heart-rate load estimate, and the mistake of charging for the rest-and-recovery features that make the gentle promise usable.

## A fitness app that won by refusing to push

Gentler Stories d.o.o. is a team of eight in Kranj, Slovenia, that previously won a 2017 Apple Design Award for a colouring-book app. The fitness app came out of two personal failures of the usual tools: CEO Katarina Lotrič was injured and could not run for months, and designer Andrej Mihelič was overtraining ("I needed something that let me know when it was enough"). Mihelič built an internal utility that "guided but it didn't push" and "wasn't based on numbers; it was more explanatory", and early TestFlight users returned at rates that made the founders ask "Are these numbers even real?" ([Apple Developer, Behind the Design](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)). It launched in February 2022, was named **Apple Watch App of the Year in November 2022**, was an ADA finalist for Visuals and Graphics in 2023, and won the ADA Social Impact award in June 2024, with Editors' Choice and App of the Day placements along the way ([FitTech Global](https://www.fittechglobal.com/fit-tech-news/Kind-activity-tracker-Gentler-Streak-wins-Apple-Watch-App-of-the-Year/350559); [Apple Developer, Behind the Design](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)). The US App Store rating stands at **4.7 from about 8,800 ratings** ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-workout-tracker/id1576857102)).

Apple's award citation is worth reading closely because it says nothing about features or hardware. It praises "thoughtful focus on both physical and mental health", an app "powered not by insistent reminders but an optimistic and encouraging vibe", a "subtle and consistently pleasing design language", and a Monthly Summary that "shows how you're doing in relation to your history" and is "less about hard comparisons and more about progress" ([Apple Design Awards 2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024)). The stated audience is "everyone else, the people who maybe didn't feel like they belonged in a gym" ([Apple Developer, Behind the Design](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)), which the founders size at "80%, or more, of the population", explicitly including people with chronic conditions, older adults, and people returning from injury, sickness or breaks ([Apptisan #025](https://apptisan.substack.com/p/apptisan-025-gentler-streak); [Sketch blog](https://www.sketch.com/blog/gentler-streak/)). That is Onward's audience too, which means Gentler Streak is proof that this segment will pay for calm. The founders' refusal to chase athletes is deliberate: competitive users are the stated non-target, and the team recently shipped a separate app, The Outsiders, for endurance athletes who want numeric readiness scores, rather than dilute the gentle product ([Help centre: Closing Out 2025](https://docs.gentler.app/release-notes-and-announcements/closing-out-2025-whats-new-in-gentler-streak-and-the-outside)).

## Three tabs, one sentence, one green band: the layout screen by screen

The app has been simplified steadily rather than expanded. Version 5.3 (March 2025) cut it from four tabs to three ([Help centre: A More Intuitive Streak](https://docs.gentler.app/release-notes-and-announcements/a-more-intuitive-streak)), and "Streak Tab 4.0" (September 2025) moved the profile badge to the top right "to reduce clutter", reduced several Go Gentler suggestions "to a single Today's Recommendation", and removed Insights from the first tab ([iThinkDiff](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/entler-streak-ios-26-update-liquid-glass/)). The founders call simplicity "the core value" and say the main screen "should stay easy to read at a glance even as deeper layers are added" ([Apptisan #025](https://apptisan.substack.com/p/apptisan-025-gentler-streak)). The current layout, assembled from the help centre and press descriptions (no screenshots were retrievable, so card order is text-derived), is:

The information hierarchy on the home screen is the real design lesson: interpretation first, picture second, one action third, numbers fourth, log last. The designer describes the top of the Streak tab (illustration plus daily status) as "a love letter from your heart" ([Sketch blog](https://www.sketch.com/blog/gentler-streak/)). The subjective inputs are a per-workout RPE (synced from Apple's Workout app since June 2025), optional notes and photos, and the occasional status switch ([9to5Mac, Jun 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/05/gentler-streak-udate-adds-step-count-rpe-sync/)). The visual style is "warm, soft, cozy, and homey", with the heart-shaped mascot Yorhart ("your heart") drawn to "resonate across age, gender, race, and body shape", and a copy voice that is "supportive but not cheesy, motivating but not fake-hyped" in everyday language ([Sketch blog](https://www.sketch.com/blog/gentler-streak/)). Accessibility (Dynamic Type, contrast, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver) was added in 2023 as a separate investment ([9to5Mac, Apr 2023](https://9to5mac.com/2023/04/06/gentler-streak-ios-accessibility-features/)).

## The band, not the chain, is why nobody feels punished

The core mechanic is the Activity Path: a green band representing "the range of activity the app considers healthy for you", computed from recent workouts (intensity, duration, frequency), overall recovery, and your manually set status, with a white dotted line showing your actual load. The lower, light-green edge is "the minimum activity needed to maintain or build fitness"; the upper, dark-green edge "warns against pushing too hard" ([Help centre: What is the Activity Path?](https://docs.gentler.app/understanding-your-activity-path/what-is-the-activity-path)). The streak is simply the dots on the 10-day path staying connected while the line stays inside the band; "the streak breaks when you move outside those levels" ([BGR, Dec 2022](https://www.bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-improves-activity-path-tracking-offers-50-off-in-black-friday-deal)). Because training load decays slowly, one idle day rarely pushes the line out, so **rest days count toward the streak by construction, not by a forgiveness token** ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)). A drag on the graph shows "a four-day prediction of where your path will land if you rest", which turns resting from a guilty guess into a visible, bounded choice ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/features/deep-dive-gentler-streak/?amp=1)). The exact formula, band-edge rules and decay rate are not published anywhere, and that opacity is part of the design: the founders refuse to show "a score percentage, or body battery concept" ([BGR, 2023](https://www.bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-apple-watch-wellbeing/)).

Two further mechanics do the emotional work. The status switch (Active / On a Break / Sick / Injured) "pauses all push notifications related to workout suggestions and activity reminders" and makes the gap "recognized as intentional rather than a lapse in motivation" ([Help centre: status](https://docs.gentler.app/personalizing-your-profile/what-happens-when-i-set-my-status-to-sick-injured-or-on-a-br)); the website's phrase for it is to "remove FOMO from sight and mind" ([Gentler Streak site](https://gentlerstories.com/gentlerstreak/)). The app earns the right to nudge by silencing itself whenever the user declares a reason not to train, and that, more than any copy choice, is the mechanism behind "no guilt-trip notifications" ([Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/gentler-streak)). The second is a single Today's Recommendation drawn from your own history and scaled to land you inside the band, with Premium users able to lengthen or shorten it with a slider; "sometimes it's gonna be rest" ([9to5Mac interview, Jun 2022](https://9to5mac.com/2022/06/25/gentler-streak-interview-watchos-9/)). Lotrič's line captures the whole product: "the world is hungry for someone to tell you that it's OK to rest, because resting is not the same as quitting" ([9to5Mac interview, Jun 2022](https://9to5mac.com/2022/06/25/gentler-streak-interview-watchos-9/)).

The research behind this is stronger than the company's own evidence, which amounts to founder anecdotes about early retention. Silverman and Barasch's 2023 experiments found broken streaks "especially demotivating" and showed that **participants who could keep a streak alive through either of two activities were more motivated than those restricted to one**, with identical actual behaviour; their design advice is to define streaks flexibly, offer repair, and never highlight a broken streak in a notification ([University of Delaware](https://lerner.udel.edu/seeing-opportunity/lerner-professor-researches-how-streaks-motivate-us/)). Duolingo, the only company to publish numbers, saw +0.38% daily actives from allowing two streak freezes and +1.7% day-7 retention from milestone animations ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)). The program-design report already established that about two-thirds of exercise-program dropouts happen in the ramp-up weeks and that a single missed day does not damage habit formation while a guilt spiral after a lapse does; Gentler Streak is the consumer product that made those findings into a screen. The pattern it shares with Apple's Pause Rings, Finch's App Pause, trainwell's grey-not-red calendar and Habitica's Pause Damage is "a streak you cannot lose by accident" ([Apple Watch User Guide](https://support.apple.com/en-bw/guide/watch/apd29b30023c/watchos); [trainwell help](https://help.trainwell.net/en/articles/985088)).

Its weaknesses are just as instructive. The load model depends on heart rate, so **yoga and strength sessions are scored too light**, users must manually raise the effort to avoid being pushed to overexert, and the developer's own docs admit that without a tracked workout "we cannot estimate the toll it took on your body" ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-workout-tracker/id1576857102); [Gentler Streak docs](https://docs.gentler.app/using-gentler-streak-on-your-apple-watch/overview-of-the-apple-watch-app-interface)). It offers "no structured workout guidance, progressive overload tracking, or coaching cues" and cannot tell you which muscle group you last trained ([Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/gentler-streak); [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-workout-tracker/id1576857102?see-all=reviews&platform=undefined)). Accurate advice "relies on consistent Apple Watch wear" ([Neura Health](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review)). A UX-writing review found the post-workout congratulations repeat until "the message stops feeling personal" ([Medium UX writing review](https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/ux-writing-review-of-gentler-streak-8babf8cb2594)). And reviewers push back on the one paywall that contradicts the philosophy: Activity Status for illness, injury or a break is Premium-only, "which arguably shouldn't be the case" ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/features/deep-dive-gentler-streak/)).

## A usable free tier, an annual plan, and Apple's editors as the marketing budget

The business is small, profitable and organic. Founders put in about €110,000 of their own money plus a modest Slovenian grant, declined investors because they "want to grow organically and sustainably", and reached **45,100 subscribers (from 5,000 in November 2022), 1,900 lifetime buyers, roughly 832,000 downloads and about 160,000 monthly users** by late 2023, all company-reported ([Mladipodjetnik.si](https://mladipodjetnik.si/novice-in-dogodki/novice/intervju-s-katarino-lotric-gentler-streak-ce-se-ne-spopades-z-napakami-ne-mores-rasti); [Startup.si](https://www.startup.si/sl-si/novica/gentler-stories-septembra-presegel-milijon-prihodkov-v-zadnjih-365-dneh)). Dividing the revenue by subscribers implies roughly €20–25 per subscriber per year, consistent with a heavily discounted annual plan after Apple's cut; the 1,900 lifetime buyers against 45,000 subscribers show lifetime is a loyalty option, not a revenue line. Growth came from Apple featured lists and pre-installed app collections, in-house PR placed with Apple-focused press on a near-monthly feature cadence, localisation into ten languages with a 15–20 person local beta per language, and an official subreddit for feedback ([Apptisan #025](https://apptisan.substack.com/p/apptisan-025-gentler-streak); [Startup.si](https://www.startup.si/sl-si/novica/gentler-stories-septembra-presegel-milijon-prihodkov-v-zadnjih-365-dneh)). Paid marketing only began in 2024, and its results are not disclosed.

Pricing has moved around a lot. The App Store today lists **$8.99 per month and $39.99 per year**, with family and lifetime tiers at several price points ($59.99–$179.99 lifetime) that look like regional or promotional variants; press over 2024–2026 reported yearly prices anywhere from $27.49 to $54.99 and a one-week free trial ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102); [TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/features/deep-dive-gentler-streak/); [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1693830/best-apple-watch-apps-health-fitness)). Black Friday and winter sales of 50% off the annual plan, and 40% off at Valentine's, recur every year ([BGR, Dec 2022](https://www.bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-improves-activity-path-tracking-offers-50-off-in-black-friday-deal)). The gating logic is consistent: the free tier shows the core promise (today's readiness, a streak, workout logging, weekly graph, monthly recap, widgets) and Premium sells the planning horizon (10- and 30-day path, the four-day rest forecast), the "life happens" status, tuning of the recommendation, notes and photos, RPE editing and long history ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/features/deep-dive-gentler-streak/?amp=1)). Free users see premium labels; paying users never do, except a renewal reminder, and get a "supporter" mark on their profile ([iThinkDiff](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/entler-streak-ios-26-update-liquid-glass/)). Lotrič told Apple that "fitness tracking, workout suggestions, metrics, and activity recaps will remain free for everyone" because "not everyone is in a position to support us" ([Apple Developer, Behind the Design](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)). The company also demonstrably listens to reviews even when a request strains the philosophy: steps were "one of the app's most requested features" and arrived in June 2025 as "a soft visual rather than a goal ring" ([9to5Mac, Jun 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/05/gentler-streak-udate-adds-step-count-rpe-sync/)).

## Verdict 1: what Gentler Streak gets right and Onward should copy

Four mechanics transfer cleanly without touching Onward's calendar. First, the **status switch**: one tap for Away, Sick or Injured that pauses notifications, labels the gap as a decision rather than a lapse, and feeds the lighter re-entry Onward already computes. It is the single most praised feature in the UX review ("really saves users from pushing beyond their limits") ([Medium UX writing review](https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/ux-writing-review-of-gentler-streak-8babf8cb2594)), and it is cheap to build. Second, the **one-sentence interpretation above everything**: Onward's new daily line is the right slot, but it should be driven by what the app knows (yesterday's feel, a gap, the phase boundary) so it reads as a message about you, not a quote of the day. Third, the **"what if I rest" preview**: the four-day forecast is Gentler Streak's answer to the question every lapsed exerciser asks, and Onward can answer it with one line because its calendar makes tomorrow certain. Fourth, **recaps that compare you only to your own past**, which is the exact thing Apple's citation singled out and the thing that makes a month of mediocre training feel like progress.

Beyond mechanics, copy the discipline. Interpretation goes above numbers; the home screen gets one action; sections get removed rather than added when newcomers get lost. Keep the no-score rule for readiness (three states and a sentence, never a percentage), because the comeback audience is the group most likely to chase or dread a number, and the Oura and Whoop complaints in the pattern research are all about score swings ([Oura support](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057791533); [JustUseApp Whoop reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/933944389/whoop/reviews)). And write a large bank of encouragement lines keyed to context, because repetition is the one documented copy complaint.

## Verdict 2: what Onward already does as well or better and should keep

Onward's structure is the thing Gentler Streak cannot offer and is explicitly criticised for lacking. Keep the **calendar-anchored 75-day program**, the **per-set weight logging**, the coded progression and reset rules, and the **short version that counts as a completed day**; the pattern research notes most consumer apps treat a scaled day as a partial, so Onward is ahead here ([trainwell help](https://help.trainwell.net/en/articles/985088)). Explicit sets, reps and effort logging sidesteps Gentler Streak's biggest recurring accuracy complaint entirely, because nothing is inferred from heart rate. The **automatic lighter re-entry after three missed days** is something only human-coached apps do well today and Gentler Streak does not do at all for structured training. The **five-habit checklist** is a self-monitoring tool with meta-analytic support that Gentler Streak has no equivalent of. **Fifteen non-resetting milestones, including "Came back"**, are a more honest version of Duolingo's milestone animations: they reward the comeback itself, which is the behaviour the product exists for. The **1–5 check-in with notes** is a subjective input Gentler Streak deliberately lacks and must infer from a wearable; for Onward it is the readiness signal that needs no Apple Watch. **Local-only data with no accounts** matches Gentler Streak's privacy positioning exactly. And the grey-not-red treatment of missed days, the "days completed of days so far" counters, and the neutral "Day 12 skipped. Day 13 is Strength B" copy already implement the Silverman advice better than a band ever could, because they never produce a visibly "broken" state.

## Verdict 3: what to deliberately not copy, and why

Do not replace the calendar with a floating band. Gentler Streak's band works because its users have no plan; Onward's users need the plan, and a band would hide the one thing a returning athlete wants to see, which is what they are lifting on Friday. Do not estimate load from heart rate; it is the root cause of the "strength scored too light" complaint and would demote the exact sessions Onward is built around. Do not ship a mascot now. Yorhart is lovely and expensive (a professional illustrator, a character that reflects state, 25 icon variants), and it signals "lifestyle app" to an audience Onward is courting as former athletes and busy parents who want to be treated as adults; Onward's rising-path mark already carries the brand. Do not add a vitals dashboard, cycle tracking, 161 workout types, or maps; they exist because Gentler Streak is a general activity tracker, and each one is a "lack of time" dropout risk in a program app. Do not keep two notification pushes a day as a default without per-notification toggles, and never let any notification mention a missed day, which is the only notification rule with peer-reviewed support ([University of Delaware](https://lerner.udel.edu/seeing-opportunity/lerner-professor-researches-how-streaks-motivate-us/)). Do not gate the status switch, the short session or the re-entry behind a paywall; that is the one Gentler Streak decision reviewers name as a mistake, and for Onward these are the tagline, not a feature. And do not copy the hidden formula: Onward's rules should stay inspectable in plain language ("all sets hit the top of the range, so next time is 5 lb more") because trust in deterministic progression is what template apps are praised for in the program-design research.

## Verdict 5: monetisation lessons for Onward's freemium

Gentler Streak's split is the right template with one correction. Keep free everything that makes the promise true: the 75-day program with swaps and the short version, the five habits, weigh-ins and the trend chart, the check-in, milestones, the status switch, re-entry, and the current program's history. These are the "core features" Apple's article credits the company for keeping free, and they are what a tester or a reviewer evaluates ([Apple Developer, Behind the Design](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)). Sell depth and automation, as the roadmap already says: adaptive coaching, custom generated programs, Apple Health and Watch integration, history beyond the current program, progress-photo compare across programs, analytics, and later family sharing. The correction is the one reviewers make about Gentler Streak: **never gate the "life happens" features** (status, short session, re-entry, skip preview), because a free user who cannot pause the app when sick is exactly the user who will churn and leave a one-star review about punishment ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/features/deep-dive-gentler-streak/)).

On price, Gentler Streak's current anchor is $8.99 monthly and about $40 annual with a 7-day trial and recurring 40–50% annual-plan sales ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102); [BGR, Dec 2022](https://www.bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-improves-activity-path-tracking-offers-50-off-in-black-friday-deal)). Onward delivers more coaching per month than a tracker but without wearable integration at launch, so pricing in the same band, with the annual plan as the lead and the 75-day length as a natural trial frame ("Pro for your first program, then decide"), is defensible. Treat lifetime as a small loyalty option (Gentler Streak's 1,900 lifetime buyers against 45,100 subscribers), show premium labels only to free users, and give paying users a supporter mark rather than more prompts ([iThinkDiff](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/entler-streak-ios-26-update-liquid-glass/)). The growth lesson matters as much as the price: the company's distribution was Apple editorial, which rewards tone, accessibility and restraint, plus monthly feature news placed with 9to5Mac-style outlets and per-language beta groups. Onward's TestFlight stage is the moment to start that cadence, and the award citation is a checklist of what to make the app look like when an editor opens it.

## Conclusion

The most useful thing this comparison changes is how to think about Onward's category. Gentler Streak proved that a calm, non-punishing app can win Apple's highest design honour and a million-euro business from "everyone else", but it did so by refusing to be a program, and the lane it left open is a structured strength comeback with Gentler Streak's manners. Onward is already in that lane: its calendar, per-set logging, short version and automatic re-entry are the features Gentler Streak's reviewers ask for and cannot get. The borrowed pieces, a status switch, a state-driven daily sentence, a skip preview and self-comparison recaps, are small builds that add Gentler Streak's emotional safety on top of Onward's structure rather than trading one for the other.

The open question is retention, which no source for either app quantifies. Gentler Streak has anecdotes; Onward will soon have ten testers. The numbers to watch are the same three the program-design report named, now with one addition: whether people are still training in week six, how often the short version rescues a day, how quickly users return after a gap, and, once the status switch ships, how many of those returns were preceded by a pause rather than a silent absence. If pausing becomes the normal way to leave, the "Missed a day? Onward" promise will have a mechanism behind it, not just a tagline.
