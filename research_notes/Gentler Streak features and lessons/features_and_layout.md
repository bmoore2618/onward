# Gentler Streak — feature set and screen-by-screen layout (research notes, Oct 2026)

Scope: what the iOS/watchOS app Gentler Streak (Gentler Stories d.o.o., Kranj, Slovenia) does and how it is laid out, as of version 5.13.x (Sept/Oct 2026). Sources are the App Store listing, the developer's help centre (docs.gentler.app), the developer's website, Apple's "Behind the Design" article (July 2024), press coverage (9to5Mac, BGR, iMore, iThinkDifferent, TapSmart), the Sketch blog interview with the designer, the Apptisan founder interview, and two third-party reviews (Neura, Lifestack) that are promotional for their own products and are flagged as such. Older sources are marked with their date; the app's layout changed materially in March 2025 (v5.3, "A More Intuitive Streak") and again in Sept 2025 ("Streak Tab 4.0", iOS 26).

Note on naming: the App Store title is "Gentler Streak Workout Tracker"; the company's website is gentlerstories.com (gentlerstreak.com redirects there). The help centre is docs.gentler.app.

## 1. Screens and navigation: main tabs, what is on each, how the home screen reads at a glance

### Takeaway
Since March 2025 the iPhone app has three tabs: **Streak** (home: daily message + Yorhart illustration, Activity Path graph, Today's Recommendation, wellness metric cards, today's and past activities), **Activities** (weekly/monthly/yearly charts and history), and **Insights** (monthly/yearly recaps and articles). Profile/settings live behind a profile badge top-right. The home screen is meant to be read at a glance: a short plain-language status sentence, a green band with a white line showing where you are in it, and one suggested action.

### Cited Findings
- Version 5.3 (released March 18, 2025) redesigned the Streak tab: "Wellbeing information moved into the For You section, reducing the app from four tabs to three. Your most relevant information appears at the top, and you can reorder or hide sections in For You." Today's logged activities appear above For You; past activities are listed below it. Menstrual cycle moved to the Streak tab, "showing your cycle day and an estimated stress tolerance." — [Gentler Streak help centre: A More Intuitive Streak](https://docs.gentler.app/release-notes-and-announcements/a-more-intuitive-streak)
- 9to5Mac on the same update: the For You section "Combines the user's Streak with health information from the Wellbeing section. It is fully customizable, so users can choose what appears at the top, such as sleep recaps in the morning and workout recaps during the day." Developer quote: "We have reimagined how the app delivers the info that matters most to you." — [9to5Mac, 18 Mar 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/03/18/gentler-streak-update-health/)
- Before v5.3 (2022–early 2025) there were four tabs; v3.0 (2023) had added a "Wellbeing" tab with seven metric cards (sleeping heart rate or resting HR, sleep duration, HRV, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, wrist temperature), each showing current value, a 10-day trendline and whether it is within normal range. — [BGR: Gentler Streak app for Apple Watch adds new Wellbeing feature](https://www.bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-apple-watch-wellbeing/); [iThinkDiff: new Wellbeing section](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/gentler-streak-new-update-wellbeing-section/) (both describe the 2023 layout, now superseded)

- Founders on glanceability: "Simplicity is the core value. The founders say the main screen should stay easy to read at a glance even as deeper layers are added." — [Apptisan #025, 29 Aug 2024](https://apptisan.substack.com/p/apptisan-025-gentler-streak)
- Step screens (v5.5, June 2025): a "soft visual" of daily steps (deliberately not a ring), current steps, distance, duration, flights, walking HR, a chart vs a typical day, a two-week calendar picker from tapping the date, a 14-day overview, walking insights. — [9to5Mac, 5 Jun 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/05/gentler-streak-udate-adds-step-count-rpe-sync/)

### Inferences
- The information hierarchy on the home screen is: (1) one sentence of interpretation, (2) the graph, (3) one action, (4) supporting metrics, (5) log. Numbers are pushed down; interpretation is pushed up.
- The app has steadily removed things from the first screen (four tabs to three; several Go Gentler suggestions to one "Today's Recommendation"; Insights off the first tab), which the team frames as making it easier for newcomers.

### Gaps
- I could not get screenshots or a pixel-level description of the current (5.13) Streak tab card order; descriptions are text-only from press and help articles.
- The exact list of "For You" sections that can be reordered/hidden is not documented in the sources I reached.

## 2. The core mechanic: Activity Path, readiness band, daily target, Go Gentler, rest days, missed days, what the streak counts

### Takeaway
The Activity Path is a green band (a personal "healthy range" of training load, computed on-device from recent workout intensity/duration/frequency, RPE, optionally daily steps, and recovery signals) with a white line showing your actual load. The daily "target" is not a number but a position: stay inside the band. Rest days inside the band keep the streak; the streak breaks only when the line leaves the band (too much or too little). Sick/Injured/On a Break statuses pause recommendations and notifications so a gap is "intentional rather than a lapse." The exact formula is not published.

### Cited Findings

**What the band is and what feeds it**
- "The Activity Path is a personalized visual guide for balancing fitness and recovery. It analyzes your workout history to recommend a daily activity range... 'a fluid recommendation that adapts daily based on your body's signals.' Inputs: Recent workouts, considering intensity, duration, and frequency; Overall recovery; Your manually set status (Active, On a Break, Sick, or Injured)." "The lower, light green edge is the minimum activity needed to maintain or build fitness. Consistently falling below it may indicate undertraining. The upper, dark green edge warns against pushing too hard. Consistently going above it suggests a risk of overtraining." — [Help centre: What is the Activity Path?](https://docs.gentler.app/understanding-your-activity-path/what-is-the-activity-path)
- Reading the graph: "Green band: the range of activity the app considers healthy for you. White dotted line: your actual activity level over time. The goal is to keep it inside the green band. Upper (dark green) area: You're nearing the edge of healthy activity. The app will likely suggest rest or light, active recovery. Middle area: good balance of training and recovery. Lower (light green) area: You're well rested and can handle a more intense workout if you want." Tips: "use it to plan your week by alternating harder and recovery days. A line that keeps dipping suggests you need more activity. A line that often touches the upper edge suggests you need more rest." — [Help centre: Interpret the Activity Path](https://docs.gentler.app/understanding-your-activity-path/interpret-the-activity-path)
- Intensity estimates use RPE: "Intensity estimates are based on the RPE (Rating of Perceived Exertion) Scale." Since v5.5 (June 2025) RPE logged in Apple's Workout app is imported and "applies it to your Activity Path. Previously, only RPE entered within Gentler Streak counted." — [9to5Mac, 5 Jun 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/05/gentler-streak-udate-adds-step-count-rpe-sync/); help article "How to Log Perceived Exertion for a Workout" exists — [Help centre index](https://docs.gentler.app/)
- Daily steps as an optional input: v5.5 "reads daily step data from Apple Health and uses it to inform your Activity Path... for people with chronic conditions or with jobs or lifestyles involving a lot of untracked walking." — [9to5Mac, 5 Jun 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/05/gentler-streak-udate-adds-step-count-rpe-sync/). The help centre has "How to Include Daily Steps in Your Activity Path Calculation", i.e. it is a toggle, and a developer reply to a review says "if that is enabled, the total number of steps should be included in the calculation." — [Help centre index](https://docs.gentler.app/); [search summary of App Store developer reply](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-workout-tracker/id1576857102)
- Manual logging feeds the path: v2.7 (2022) added "Add Workout Manually" with type, duration, intensity and distance; manual entries "show on the path alongside tracked ones." — [9to5Mac, 12 Oct 2022](https://9to5mac.com/2022/10/12/gentler-streak-apple-watch-update/)
- The founders explicitly avoid a readiness number: the developers "avoid putting your daily form into a score percentage, or body battery concept." — [BGR: Wellbeing feature (2023)](https://www.bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-apple-watch-wellbeing/)
- Early (2022) description: "The path shows your optimal activity levels and a training log for the past 10 days. Levels are based on logged workouts and their effect on your performance." — [iMore, 31 Dec 2022](https://imore.com/apps/health-fitness-apps/want-to-hit-your-2023-fitness-goals-drop-apples-rings-and-try-gentler-streak-instead)
- Third-party reviews (promotional, treat as secondary): Neura says the app "Uses last night's sleep and this morning's resting heart rate to estimate how ready you are to train"; the band "Dips after several hard days to suggest easier training. Slopes upward after about a week of inactivity to encourage more movement." Lifestack lists inputs as "HRV trends, resting heart rate, sleep, and recent workout load" and says the app "needs about 3 to 4 weeks of data before recommendations feel accurate." — [Neura hands-on review, 5 Oct 2026](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review); [Lifestack blog, Sep/Oct 2026](https://lifestack.ai/blog/gentler-streak). Caveat: the developer's own help article lists only workouts, recovery and status as Activity Path inputs; the vitals (sleep, HRV, RHR) are documented as feeding the "Daily Readiness" summary and recommendations, not explicitly the band. The precise weighting is not published anywhere I found.

### Inferences
- "Missed day without streak reset" is achieved structurally, not by forgiveness tokens: because the metric is a load band rather than a daily action, one idle day usually keeps the line inside the band (load decays slowly), so nothing visibly breaks. Only a sustained stretch below the lower edge breaks it, and the app frames that as "needs more activity," not failure.
- The band itself slopes: after hard days the upper edge is closer (so rest is "in the green"); after long inactivity the band is low and gently rising, so a short walk re-enters it. This matches the help centre's "fluid recommendation" and the Neura description, but the slope rule is inferred, not documented.
- The 10-day window is the working horizon; the 4-day rest forecast is the key "what if I do nothing" affordance that removes guessing.

### Gaps
- No public formula: how training load is scored from HR zones/RPE/duration, how band edges are set, how fast load decays, how sleep/HRV/RHR shift the band vs. only the daily message. The help centre says "analyzes your workout history" and lists qualitative inputs only.
- Exact rule for when the streak visually "breaks" (how many consecutive days outside, whether above-band breaks it the same as below) is not documented beyond "when you fall outside."
- Whether the streak is shown as a day count anywhere (e.g. "12-day streak") is unclear; sources describe connected dots, not a counter.
- How status affects the band's position on return (e.g. a lowered band after illness) is not documented.

## 3. Inputs and integrations: Apple Health, Apple Watch app and complications, manual logging, check-ins, mood/notes, sleep, recovery, cycle, weather

### Takeaway
Everything comes from Apple Health (workouts, HR, HRV, sleep, steps, vitals, cycle) and the app writes workouts back; processing is on-device with no accounts or servers. There is no explicit "how do you feel" slider; the subjective input is RPE per workout (synced from Apple's Workout app), workout notes/photos, and the manual Active/Sick/Injured/On a Break status. No weather integration was found. Garmin workouts come in via Health.

- Privacy/architecture: "health and fitness data stays on the device through Apple HealthKit, with no external processing, and that there are no servers or accounts." — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102); website: "Apple Health: Reads and writes data in both directions... No user accounts and no servers. Personal data is not shared with AI models or ad platforms." — [gentlerstories.com/gentlerstreak](https://gentlerstories.com/gentlerstreak/)
- Workout tracking: "140+ activities with maps and metric charts" (App Store); 161 types after v5.12.5 (June 2026) added 13. "Workout journal: Renamable summaries, notes, photos, favorites, and an RPE chart." — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)
- Daily Chores activity: "An activity option that counts housework, such as dog-walking or chores, toward activity" (premium, per a user review). — [gentlerstories.com/gentlerstreak](https://gentlerstories.com/gentlerstreak/)

## 4. Visual style, copy voice, notifications, onboarding

### Takeaway
Warm, soft, illustrated UI with a heart-shaped mascot (Yorhart, "your heart") that reflects the user's state; copy is plain-language, supportive, "guides but doesn't push." Notifications are few and categorised (status reminders, workout summary, recaps, tips, offers; a Morning Check-In category added June 2026), and all workout-suggestion pushes pause while a status is set. Onboarding is short (Health permissions, personal details) and reportedly under five minutes.

## 8. Mechanics worth borrowing for a "comeback" app (synthesis for the product team)

### Takeaway
The transferable ideas are: a band instead of a target; one-sentence daily interpretation above the graph; a single recommended action with a duration slider; rest inside the band counts; an explicit "life happens" status that silences nudges and re-labels the gap as intentional; a "what if I rest" forecast; recaps that compare you with your own past; no daily questionnaire.

### Cited Findings
- Band not target; rest counts; status silences reminders; forecast on drag; single Today's Recommendation; plain-language daily message; self-comparison recaps — all cited in sections 1, 2, 4 above (help centre, App Store, Apple Behind the Design, TapSmart, iThinkDiff).
- Design constraint stated by founders: "the main screen should stay easy to read at a glance even as deeper layers are added." — [Apptisan #025](https://apptisan.substack.com/p/apptisan-025-gentler-streak)
- Known weakness relevant to Onward: "No structured workout guidance, progressive overload tracking, or coaching cues"; "Likely too flexible for competitive athletes who need strict schedules." — [Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/gentler-streak); [Neura](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review)

### Inferences
- Onward's calendar-anchored program (Day N = fixed date, missed day skipped) is the opposite choice from Gentler Streak's floating band; the Gentler Streak pieces that fit Onward without changing that decision are the status switch (sick/injured/away) that pauses notifications and lowers the next session, the one-line interpretation at the top of Today, and showing "what happens if you rest" rather than a broken chain.

### Gaps
- No source gives retention or conversion figures that would show which of these mechanics drives outcomes.
