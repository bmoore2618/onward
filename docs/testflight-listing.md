## Beta App Description

Onward is a 75-day comeback program for people getting back into shape after time away. Three short strength sessions, two conditioning days, one easy day and one rest day each week, plus five daily habits. Miss a day and nothing resets: the plan simply continues, and the first session back is lighter on purpose.

Choose where you train (home gym, full gym, bodyweight or cardio only) and anything to work around (lower back, knees, shoulders), and the app fills each session to match.

## What to Test

Thanks for testing. Please try:

- The setup screens on first open. Was anything confusing?
- Logging a full day: the session, weights, the five habits, then Complete Day.
- Missing a day or two on purpose. How does the app treat you when you come back?
- The Progress tab after a few days: calendar, body weight, milestones.

Send feedback from Settings → Send feedback, or take a screenshot and use TestFlight's Share Beta Feedback.
