# Onward privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Onward is a fitness and habit app. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone, and we can't see it.

## What the app stores

Onward keeps the following on your device only:

- Your setup answers, such as first name, Day 1 date, equipment, goal weight and reminder times.
- Your training log: days completed, checklist ticks, weights lifted, movement swaps, self-test results and notes.
- Your weigh-ins and daily check-ins.
- Progress photos you choose to take or add. The app saves its own copies in its private storage on your phone.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync. We have no server that holds your data.

## Camera and photos

Onward asks for camera and photo library access only so you can add progress photos. It reads only the photos you pick. You can turn this access off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. They are not sent from a server, and we do not receive any information about them.

## Backups

If you use Export backup in Settings, the app creates a file with your data and photos and opens the iPhone share sheet. Where that file goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive or email, is your choice and is handled by Apple or the service you pick. Restore reads only the file you choose.

## App updates

To deliver bug fixes and improvements, the app checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) when it opens. That request includes technical details needed to pick the right update, such as the app version, the platform and your IP address. It does not include anything you have entered in the app. Expo's handling of that request is covered by Expo's privacy policy at https://expo.dev/privacy.

## Demo videos

Tapping "Watch demo" opens the video on YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app. YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

## TestFlight

If you are testing Onward through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight with us. Apple's TestFlight terms describe what is included.

## Analytics and advertising

Onward has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party data sharing.

## Health information

Onward is a fitness and habit tracker, not a medical service. It does not read from or write to Apple Health. Body weight and how you felt are things you type in yourself, and they stay on your phone.

## Children

Onward is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your phone deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts storing data off your phone, for example with accounts or sync, this page will say so before that feature ships.
